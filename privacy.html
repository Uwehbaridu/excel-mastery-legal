Privacy Policy

Excel Formula Mastery. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

This policy explains what the Excel Formula Mastery app ("the app") collects and how it is used.

What stays on your device

Your lesson progress, practice results, hearts, streaks and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them unless you sign in (see below).

Signing in

You can use the app as a guest. If you choose "Continue with Google", Google and Firebase Authentication give us your name, email address, profile photo and a unique account ID. We use these only to identify your account and show your name in the app.

Ads

The free parts of the app show ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect your device's advertising ID and similar information to show and measure ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings.

Purchases

Hearts and Pro plans are bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles your payment details; we never see your card number. We receive confirmation of what you bought so we can unlock it.

What we do not do

AI features (Ask Bright, Ask AI and job interview practice)

When you use Ask Bright, Ask AI on a function page, or the job interview practice, what you type, what you say, and any job description you paste in are sent over the internet to Google's Gemini AI service, through Firebase, so it can reply to you and score your practice. Google processes this content to produce the reply under Google's Privacy Policy.

The microphone is used only during a live voice call with Ask Bright, and only while the call is open. Your voice is streamed to the AI service for the call; the app does not save audio recordings. The app asks for microphone permission the first time you start a voice call, and you can switch it off in your Android settings. Typed chat works without the microphone.

We do not use your conversations to build advertising profiles. A short summary of your practice results and daily usage counts are kept on your device. AI answers can be wrong, so check important formulas in Excel. Please do not enter passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information into the AI features.

Security checks

To keep the AI service from being abused, the app uses Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, which sends Google a signal that the app is genuine and running on a real device.

Other

We do not sell your personal information. The app does not use your contacts, location or camera.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time, and you can ask us to delete your account data by contacting us at the address below. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
